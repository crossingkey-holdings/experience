# Reviewer Guide

This repository is designed to let a reviewer assess my work quickly without searching across dozens of projects.

## AI systems, evaluation, or agent roles

Read `docs/GOVERNED-EXECUTION.md`, `docs/HUMAN-AI-INTERACTION.md`, `samples/evaluation-rubric.md`, and `PROJECTS.md`.

Look for instruction following, context preservation, model-output evaluation, bounded tool execution, failure handling, human approval boundaries, inspectable state, and verification.

## Software, automation, or infrastructure roles

Read `TECHNICAL-SKILLS.md`, `PROJECTS.md`, and `samples/governed-agent-loop.py`, then inspect the linked public repositories in `README.md`.

## Contract, partnership, or acquisition diligence

Read `PROJECTS.md`, `EXPERIENCE.md`, `PRIVATE-ACCESS.md`, and `PUBLIC-SAFETY.md`.

The public repository is intentionally scoped. Private source, commercial infrastructure, unreleased research, customer data, credentials, and security-sensitive implementation detail belong in a controlled diligence process.
